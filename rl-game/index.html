Get Out the Vote

You're a canvasser. Start at the campaign office (H). The polls close in 25 moves. Two houses (S) are our supporters; one (O) supports the opponent. Step onto a house to knock: that voter goes out to vote, once. Our supporters' votes help us. The opponent's supporter's vote hurts us. The game ends when the polls close, or as soon as both of our supporters have voted. Then a Q-learning agent learns the same game from the points alone.
