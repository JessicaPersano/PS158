About

Jessica Persano is a Pre-Doctoral Research Fellow at Stanford GSB, working under Professor Andy Hall in the Free Systems Lab. She graduated from UCLA with double majors in political science (honors) and geography/environmental studies, and is interested in pursuing a PhD in political science, studying American political institutions and local election administration.

This quarter she's taking POLISCI 158 to understand the technical side of how AI functions, in order to better understand its behavior.

PS158 project interests

She's interested in AI's impact on elections and representation, and has experience auditing the political capabilities of AI and studying AI's ability to do empirical research. She'd like to work on questions like:

  • How do LLMs represent constituent interests? Have a model predict county- or district-level results for past ballot measures, then see whether its errors follow partisanship, region, or rurality.
  • The political economy of state AI laws Use an LLM to code 2023–26 state AI bills by whose interests they protect, then ask whether industry presence in a state predicts which bills pass.
  • AI as a simulated legislature Build an agent for each member of one state legislature from their past votes, district, and statements, and score its votes against this session's real roll calls.
  • Forecasting the 2026 midterms/ballot measures Before Nov 3, have several models forecast county-level results for statewide ballot measures, then score them against the actual returns.
